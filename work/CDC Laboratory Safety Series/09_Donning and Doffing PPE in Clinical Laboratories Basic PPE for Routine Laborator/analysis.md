The strategy demonstrated in this video is to remove PPE from most contaminated to least contaminated. To doff your PPE, the first step is to slowly and gently remove your gloves. Start by making an L with your thumb and first finger. Then, place your fingers perpendicular to your wrist and pinch. Be careful to not touch the cuff of the coat with your dirty glove. Next, pull the glove completely off. It should now be inside out. Grab the inside-out glove with your bare hand, the one the glove just came off of, and then pinch the other glove at the wrist. Then, pull the pinched glove up high and away from the wrist. Pull the glove off, making sure the gloved hand is flat and you do not touch the outside of the contaminated glove with your clean hand. |
