To anyone who is new, thank you for joining us today. My name is Ava Casper, and today I will be talking to you all about CO2. For anyone unfamiliar with what that is, it stands for carbon dioxide. Now, I know this topic may give some of you guys the heebie jeebies, but it is an often discussed topic both inside and outside of our industry. Usually the discussion concerns the negatives of CO2 and how it affects Earth's climate by releasing emissions into the atmosphere. But did you know CO2 plays such an important role in our everyday lives? According to CGA, some of the most biggest and critical applications being used with CO2 are water treatment, medical, infrastructure, food, beverage, and agriculture. So what does that mean? It means the carbonation in your drinks is thanks to CO2, as well as keeping food and drink items stored cold because of dry ice. Current vaccines that are used day-to-day need to be stored at cold temperatures, and CO2 is used for vaccines to be stored safely and properly. Also, CO2 increases the growth of crops and benefits the levels in plants. These are just a few examples of how CO2 is benefited, and the list can just keep going on and on. The sole purpose of our Equi-Tip today was to inform you of some of the positive impacts of CO2. There is no reason you should be spooked by CO2. If you are interested in some guidelines regarding CO2 hazard management, take a look at the CGA posters linked below.
