These are a vacuum line to reduce the pressure inside of the whole rotabap system. Remember, solvents boil at lower temperatures under reduced atmosphere. A hot water bath used to warm the flask containing your dissolved material. Evaporation is a cooling process, so you need to add a little heat to evaporate the solvent. A cooled condenser to condense the solvent vapor from your flask and a receiver to collect the condensed solvent. If all goes well, the solvent that starts out in your flask ends up in the receiver while your material stays put in your flask.
