- 原片：Lesson 6: How to Cut Long Materials Using Passthrough?（厂商系列课第 6 课，11 分 13 秒，英语讲解）；内容文档：`course/Cleanroom & Equipment Safety/02_Lesson 6 How to Cut Long Materials Using Passthrough？.md`
